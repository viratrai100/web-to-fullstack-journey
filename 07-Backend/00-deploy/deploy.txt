Netlify aur Vercel jaise pure free-unlimited nahi hote Render, Railway, AWS, DigitalOcean — unme mostly free tier (trial) milta hai, jisme thoda limit hota hai 👇

1. Render

Free Tier: ✅ haan, backend + frontend dono free deploy kar sakte ho

Limits:

750 free hours per month (≈ 1 service running full month)

512 MB RAM

Sleep ho jaata hai agar traffic na aaye

2. Railway

Free Tier: ✅ haan

Limits:

$5 credit per month (matlab chhote project free chal jaayenge)

Thoda usage cross karte hi paid plan chahiye

3. AWS (Amazon Web Services)

Free Tier: ✅ haan, par 1 year ke liye hi free

Limits:

750 hours EC2 (server)

5GB S3 storage, 30GB database

12 months ke baad → paid

4. DigitalOcean

Free Tier: ❌ No permanent free

✅ Lekin $200 free credit dete hain 60 days ke liye

Uske baad paid karna padta hai

Shortcut Rule

Free unlimited frontend → Netlify, Vercel

Free fullstack (with limits) → Render, Railway

Trial only (1 yr ya 60 days) → AWS, DigitalOcean








“750 free hours per month” ka matlab ye hai 👇

1 mahine me ~30 din hote hain → 30 × 24 = 720 ghante

Agar koi service (jaise backend server) non-stop 24/7 chalti rahe, to usko ek poore mahine ke liye ~720 ghante chahiye.

To jab koi platform bolta hai 750 free hours/month, iska matlab:
👉 Tum ek service ko poore mahine 24/7 free chala sakte ho.
👉 Ya phir multiple services thode-thode time ke liye chalaoge to sabka total 750 hours free milega.

Example:

Ek project full month chalayoge → covered.

Do projects chalayoge → dono ko half-half month free milega (kyunki total 750 hours hi free hai).



⚡ Compare karke dekho:

Render → Easy, free tier, beginners ke liye best.

Railway → Similar hai Render jaise, thoda developer-friendly UI.

AWS / DigitalOcean → Zyada powerful, but beginner ke liye complex + billing details chahiye.

Vercel / Netlify → Sirf frontend (React, Next.js, static sites) ke liye best, backend limited.

🔑 Conclusion:
👉 Ek project ke liye → Render best hai.
👉 Agar professional / large scale project banega future me → AWS ya DigitalOcean sochna.
